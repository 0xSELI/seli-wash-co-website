# SELI Wash Co. Website — Project Brief

## Project status

Concept stage. A visual direction is approved and the core business facts are supplied. The website should not be built until the hosting and form approach is chosen and the owner explicitly asks for implementation.

Business facts live in `BUSINESS_INPUTS.md`, which is the authority. This brief describes intent; where the two differ, `BUSINESS_INPUTS.md` wins.

## Name and identity

- **Public name:** SELI Wash Co.
- **Tagline:** Pressure Washing & Exterior Cleaning
- **Legal structure:** not registered. No LLC or other suffix anywhere.
- **Domain:** `seliwash.com` proposed; purchase and control unconfirmed.
- **Logo:** none. A text wordmark stands in.

Note that the tagline is broader than the launch scope, which is concrete flatwork only. Either narrow the tagline or expect enquiries for services that will be turned down.

## Purpose

Create a trustworthy, conversion-focused website for a new pressure washing company. The site should explain the services clearly, show the visible difference the work makes, and turn local visitors into estimate requests.

## Primary audiences

1. Homeowners who want driveways, sidewalks, patios, and other exterior surfaces cleaned.
2. Property managers who need dependable recurring or one-time cleaning.
3. Owners or managers of parking areas and other small commercial properties.

## Core customer promise

Make outdoor surfaces look clean, cared for, and close to new through professional pressure washing and straightforward service.

The final wording must avoid guarantees that the company cannot consistently support.

## Business goals

- Generate qualified local quote requests.
- Make calling or contacting the company effortless on mobile.
- Build trust for a new business through transparent expectations and clear service information. There are no reviews, credentials, or completed-work photos yet, so trust must come from honesty about that rather than from borrowed signals.
- Explain estimated pricing without promising a final price before the job is evaluated.
- Support both residential customers and property managers without making the experience feel corporate or impersonal.
- Establish a foundation for local search visibility.

## Primary conversion paths

1. Visitor selects **Request a Free Estimate**, enters the property and job details, confirms an outdoor spigot and GFCI outlet are within reach, optionally attaches photos, and submits a request.
2. Visitor selects **Text for an Estimate** and messages **(580) 560-9673** directly. Text is the owner's preferred channel and often the fastest path to a real conversation.
3. Mobile visitor taps the persistent call or text button.

There is **no online booking**. Every request is confirmed by hand, so the site must never present a slot as reserved, imply instant confirmation, or promise a response time — none has been set.

## Required pages

### Home

- Clear headline, service area, and primary call to action.
- Short overview of the most common services.
- Before-and-after proof.
- Factual at-a-glance details (service area, work days, contact, site requirements) and a simple explanation of the process. A reviews block only once real reviews exist.
- Service-area preview, frequently asked questions, and final quote call to action.

### Services and pricing estimates

- Individual service categories with inclusions and exclusions.
- Starting prices or estimate ranges where the owner can support them.
- A visible explanation that size, buildup and staining, access, surface condition, specialty treatment, and travel may affect the final quote, always beside the $50 standalone minimum.
- Residential services first, with a clear section for property managers and parking areas.
- A quote call to action beside each major service.

**Confirmed launch services** are concrete flatwork only: driveways, sidewalks and walkways, patios and porches, and steps and entry pads. Parking pads and aprons plus recurring property care are custom-quote.

**Confirmed as not offered,** and not to be advertised: roof washing, house or siding soft washing, gutters, fences, decks, sealing, joint-sand replacement, restoration, pavers, brick, pool decks, and any decorative, painted, coated, sealed, stamped, or fragile surface.

### Gallery

- Real before-and-after project pairs, preferably with a comparison slider.
- Filters by surface or service when enough projects exist.
- Short captions describing the surface and work completed.
- No stock imagery presented as completed company work.

### About

- Founder or company story. **Not yet supplied.**
- Service values: reliability, care for the property, clear communication, and professional results.
- **No credentials exist.** There is no insurance, licence, registration, certification, training, or guarantee. None may be claimed, implied, or hinted at, and the absence must not be dressed up with reassuring copy.
- A friendly photo of the owner or team when available. **None yet.**

### Contact

- Quote-request form.
- Business phone (call and text), email, phone-answering hours, and work days. **No response-time expectation** is published until the owner sets one.
- Service-area map or list.
- No booking widget. The estimate request is the only submission path.
- Privacy notice and consent language for submitted information.

## Important features

### Launch essentials

- Mobile-friendly estimate form with name, contact details, address or ZIP code, requested service, approximate size, preferred timing, and notes. It must also ask, in plain language, whether an outdoor water spigot and a working outdoor GFCI outlet are within about 25 feet of the area — a no means the job likely cannot be done, and that is cheaper to learn on the form than in the driveway. Photo upload only if reliably supported; otherwise tell customers to text photos.
- Before-and-after gallery.
- Service-area map centred on Duncan, Oklahoma, and a plain statement of the $5-per-additional-mile travel charge outside it. No other towns are confirmed, so none are listed.
- Testimonials or review cards only once real, permitted, sourced reviews exist.
- Estimate-request flow with manual confirmation. No scheduling integration at launch.
- Click-to-call and click-to-email actions.
- Clear pricing-estimate disclaimer.
- Frequently asked questions covering preparation, water access, timing, safety, and what affects price.
- Confirmation and error states for every form.
- Spam protection and secure handling of customer information.
- Basic local SEO: descriptive page titles, local business information, social-sharing metadata, sitemap, and LocalBusiness structured data when details are verified.
- Accessibility support, fast mobile performance, reduced-motion support, and keyboard-friendly interactions.
- Privacy policy and terms or service conditions appropriate to the selected forms and integrations.

### Strong additions after launch

- Google Business Profile and review links.
- Automated email or text confirmation after a quote request.
- Simple lead tracking for source, status, and follow-up.
- Project-area pages only when they contain useful, original local content.
- Maintenance-plan or recurring-service inquiry for property managers.
- Referral or seasonal promotion section.
- Analytics and conversion tracking after a privacy-conscious tool is selected.

## Recommended launch scope

The first release should contain the five required pages, the quote form, a service-area section, real gallery content, FAQs, and essential legal/privacy information. Advanced customer accounts, instant pricing calculators, payments, and complicated scheduling should wait until the company has validated its workflow.

A reviews section ships only if real reviews exist by then. If none do, the section is either omitted or states plainly that the business is new — it is never filled with samples.

## Early success measures

- Qualified quote requests per month.
- Calls and texts originating from the website.
- Quote-form completion rate.
- Percentage of leads located inside the service area.
- Mobile page speed and accessibility quality.
- Search impressions for relevant local services after indexing.

Numeric targets should be set after the first month of usable traffic provides a baseline.

## Product principles

- Trust before spectacle.
- Real work before stock imagery.
- One obvious next action on every page.
- Transparent estimates without misleading certainty.
- Mobile usability is the priority.
- Animation should support the story and never slow down or obscure the conversion path.

## Out of scope for this planning phase

- Website code or framework setup.
- Final visual mockups.
- Logo or brand identity production.
- Hosting, domain configuration, deployment, or third-party integrations.
- Publishing unverified prices, reviews, credentials, or service claims.
